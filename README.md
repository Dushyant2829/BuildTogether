# BuildTogether

Learn by building. Not by watching.

---

## 📌 Description

BuildTogether is a collaborative learning platform designed to bridge the gap between theory and real-world project building. Instead of passive learning through recorded tutorials, it enables learners to actively participate in live project-building sessions led by experienced builders. The platform connects two types of users: builders, who host and guide projects, and learners, who join these sessions to gain hands-on experience.

Builders can create structured project sessions, define learning outcomes, set participation limits, and choose whether their sessions are free or paid. Learners can browse available projects, join sessions based on their interests, and build alongside the instructor in real time. This approach transforms learning into an interactive and engaging process, where participants not only understand concepts but also apply them practically.

A key feature of BuildTogether is its focus on “learning by doing.” By working on real projects collaboratively, learners develop practical skills, improve problem-solving abilities, and gain confidence in their capabilities. Upon completing a session, learners receive a certification that validates their hands-on experience, which can be showcased in portfolios or resumes.

The platform also fosters a community-driven ecosystem where knowledge sharing is encouraged. Builders gain recognition and potential income, while learners gain mentorship and real-world exposure.

BuildTogether aims to redefine online education by shifting from passive content consumption to active skill development, making learning more effective, accessible, and outcome-driven.
